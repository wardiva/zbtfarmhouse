OUR STORY

ZBT Farmhouse Faisalabad

ZBT Farmhouse Faisalabad is a premium private farmhouse and event venue located near Wapda City, Faisalabad. Designed to offer a luxurious yet peaceful escape from the city's hustle and bustle, it provides an ideal setting for family picnics, private pool parties, birthdays, weddings, BBQ nights, corporate gatherings, bridal showers, and photoshoots.

The farmhouse features a crystal-clear private swimming pool, scenic lake views, lush green lawns, a wooden gazebo, an air-conditioned TV lounge, a fully equipped kitchen, guest rooms, kids' play area, spacious event lawns, and secure parking. Combining resort-style amenities with complete privacy, ZBT Farmhouse delivers a comfortable and memorable experience for families, friends, and event organizers alike.

Zubaida Barkat Trust

ZBT Farmhouse is a project of Zubaida Barkat Trust, and 25% of the farm income goes to www.zbt.org.pk, which is located in Tarnol, Islamabad. Shafqat Iron Store Faisalabad started this project for the development of Faisalabad, as there are limited entertainment opportunities in town, so ZBT farmhouse offers you all the club facilities without giving a purchasing a lifelong membership fee that is a huge amount on your pockets.

Shafqat Iron Store

Shafqat Iron & Cement Store is a building materials supplier in Faisalabad specializing in iron and cement products for construction projects. They have sole distribution of Amerli Steels. Also they have other brands like FF Steels, Hunza Steels, Mughal Steels, Farooq Steels, T-R gader, Pioneer cement, Askari cement & maple cement.
