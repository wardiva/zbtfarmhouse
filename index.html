RESORT GUIDELINES

ZBT Resort Rules & Regulations

To ensure maximum comfort, hygiene, and safety for all guests, please follow our resort rules.

Strict Slot Timing

Guests are requested to adhere strictly to booked check-in and check-out slot timings (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).

Pool Hygiene & Costumes

Proper swimwear is mandatory. Showering before entering the chlorinated pool is required for water cleanliness.

25%-50% Advance Deposit

Slot reservations are only locked upon receipt of 25% to 50% advance deposit via online transfer.

Family Gated Privacy

Zero outsider entry allowed. High perimeter walls and locked gates ensure 100% private security.

500-Car Secure Parking

Sound & Music Limits

Outdoor high-volume sound systems must be moderated after 11:00 PM as per local resort guidelines.
