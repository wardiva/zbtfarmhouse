Data Privacy & Tracking Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ZBT Farmhouse Faisalabad. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, when participating in activities on the website, or when contacting us.

  • Name and Contact Data (Phone number, email address)
  • Booking Details and Preferences

3. Meta (Facebook) & Google Ads Pixel Tracking

We use marketing and tracking technologies, specifically the Meta Pixel (formerly Facebook Pixel) and Google Ads Conversion Tracking, to help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and understand how visitors interact with our website.

How We Use Tracking Data

The data collected via pixels allows us to:

  • Track conversions from our Meta and Google advertisements.
  • Build targeted audience lists for future advertising (Retargeting).
  • Optimize our ads based on user behavior on this website.

The data collected is completely anonymous to us. We do not see personal data of individual users through these platforms. However, Meta and Google may store this data and use it for their own promotional purposes according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Consent to Tracking

By continuing to browse and use our website, you consent to the use of these tracking pixels. If you wish to opt-out, you can disable cookies in your browser settings or use ad-blocking software that prevents tracking pixels from loading.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email us at zbtfarm@gmail.com or WhatsApp us at 0333-8666964.
